The arc

From a virtual-only practice to a rated, reviewed, physical clinic.

  • November 2023

    Engagement begins. Connected Minds is virtual-only; the work starts with rebuilding and managing their website.

  • Spring – Summer 2024

    Market and competitive research for a physical location: demographics, five nearby competitors mapped, a location-selection recommendation built and delivered.

  • August – September 2024

    Marketing plan built for the new Marietta location: SEO and landing pages for TMS and Spravato, geo-targeted search, social, email, and a reputation-management program.

  • October 7, 2024

    Grand opening at 2155 Post Oak Tritt Road, Suite 300, Marietta, GA: a ribbon-cutting, a TMS demonstration, and the first physical home the practice has had.

  • Ongoing

    Search, social, video, press, out-of-home, and direct mail all run continuously. The clinic reports their website as a primary source of new business.

What's running now

Search

SEO and paid search targeted on TMS, Spravato, and general mental-health-care queries within a 10–15 mile radius of Marietta.

Social & video

Meta Ads across Facebook and Instagram, plus produced video content and short-form reels built around patient-education topics and clinic updates.

Press

A formal press release announced the Marietta location's grand opening and expanded services, distributed alongside the launch marketing push.

Direct mail

A geo-targeted postcard sequence mailed to the neighborhoods around the new location, the same area-farming approach used to build local awareness before a launch.

Out-of-home & events

Billboard placements and physical events, including the grand-opening ribbon-cutting and ongoing community sessions.

Reputation

Active review generation and monitoring. The foundation under the 5.0 rating and the BusinessRate recognition.

The lead signal

The clinic's appointment-request form had logged 799 submissions as of September 2026. This isn't a launch spike that faded: the first full month ran 33 requests, and two years later the form is still running dozens of submissions a month. Medication management for an existing diagnosis has been the single most-requested service in almost every period since launch, which has held steady as the clearest signal of what the market actually wants from this clinic.
